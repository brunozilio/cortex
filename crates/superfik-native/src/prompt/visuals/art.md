# Illustration and generative art

The same SVG setup applies (width 440, safe area, one SVG), with a different aesthetic:
- Fill the canvas: art should feel rich, not sparse. A full-bleed background shape is allowed here.
- Bold colour: mix the ramps freely, or choose your own palette in hard-coded hex. Pick colours that hold up on
  both a light and a dark conversation, or paint your own background behind them.
- Depth from overlapping opaque shapes; organic forms with `<path>` curves, `<ellipse>` and `<circle>`.
- Texture by repetition (parallel lines, dots, hatching), never raster effects or filters.
- Radial symmetry with `<g transform="rotate(…)">`.
- No text unless the piece needs it.
